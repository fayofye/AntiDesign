Buttons

btn-01 Rubber stamp

Use it when: an approve, sign-off or mark-as-done action should feel final and leave a mark. Change: the stamp word, ink and paper colours, the face; keep the random seed and tilt so no two stamps match.

Sign-off

btn-02 Ticket stub

Use it when: claiming a seat, an RSVP or a one-time entry, where the act is irreversible. Change: the ticket words and colours, the stub copy, the fine print; keep the half-holes on both torn edges.

btn-03 Pull chain

Use it when: a two-state switch (dark mode, a feature on/off) should be pulled, not ticked. Drag the chain down, or click, or press Enter. Change: the room colours, the tag word, the lamp; keep the switch firing at the bottom of the pull.

Off

btn-04 Keycap

Use it when: an action has a keyboard shortcut worth teaching (save, next, search). The key travels into the plate and fires from its shortcut too. Change: the legend, the shortcut letter, the cap and plate colours.

Click the key or press S

btn-05 Wax seal

Use it when: opening something private or ceremonial: an invitation, a result, a reveal. Change: the wax colour, the monogram, the envelope and letter copy.

You're invited

btn-06 Fuse

Use it when: a destructive action needs a beat of intent (delete, cancel a plan, wipe data). Hold the pointer, Space or Enter until the fuse burns down; let go early and it goes out. Clicking twice also works. Change: the action word, the hold time (HOLD), the colours.

Hold to delete, or click it twice. Sample: nothing is removed.

btn-07 Price tag

Use it when: add-to-bag or save-for-later on a shop that sells physical goods. The tag swings on its string like a pendulum when brushed or clicked. Change: the tag copy, the twine colours, the cloth behind; keep the swing short and damped.

btn-08 Split-flap

Use it when: booking, check-in or any status that changes, on a travel, transport or events product. Each letter turns forward through the drum until it lands. Change: the two words (same length), the board colours, the drum order.

btn-09 Elevator call

Use it when: joining a queue, a waitlist or requesting a callback, where the wait itself is the feedback. Change: the floors (FLOORS), the LED colour, the panel; label the countdown as sample unless it is real.

btn-10 Sticker peel

Use it when: revealing a code, a reward or a hidden answer. The corner lifts on hover or focus; a click peels it off. Change: the sticker colour and copy, the hidden code, the surface behind.

Code: SAMPLE-CODE

btn-11 Ballot mark

Use it when: a vote, a poll or a one-of-several choice, where picking should feel like marking paper. Works without JavaScript. Change: the options, the paper and pencil colours, the header.

SAMPLE BALLOT

btn-12 Safety cover

Use it when: a high-stakes switch (publish, go live, send to everyone) should take two deliberate steps. Lift the cover, then flip the switch; closing the cover pushes the switch back to off. Change: the action word, the stripes, the panel colour.

OFF

btn-13 Signature

Use it when: sign up, accept or agree, where a signature is the honest metaphor. The words write themselves with a pen. Change: the signed words, the ink and card colours, the line of terms.

By signing you agree to the sample terms.

btn-14 Tally

Use it when: an upvote, a like or a counter where the count should look counted by hand. Every fifth stroke crosses the four before it. Change: the board and chalk colours, the label, the cap (MAX).

0 votes

btn-15 Zipper

Use it when: a disclosure (details, specs, more info) on a fashion, bag or outdoor product. Drag the pull along the teeth, or click it, or press Enter. Change: the fabric and lining colours, the label, the details copy.

Unzip for details

Sample details: size, material, care. Replace with the product's own.

btn-16 Piano keys

Use it when: a segmented choice (view, mode, speed) on a product with some hardware in its soul. Pressing one key releases the other, like a tape deck's latch bar. Works without JavaScript. Change: the options, the body colour, the key count.

View

btn-17 Embroidered patch

Use it when: join, follow or subscribe on a club, team or community product. Pressing sews the patch on, one stitch at a time; pressing again unpicks it. Change: the word, the thread and canvas colours, the patch shape.
