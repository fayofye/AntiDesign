Motion

Specimens to re-cut, never to ship as they are: change the copy, colours and type to the product's own. Roll for one with python3 scripts/roll.py --library motion.

mo-01 Odometer

Use it when: a running total (distance, orders, members) should feel counted by a machine, not announced. Change: FROM and TO (in tenths), the unit line, the drum colours.

mo-02 Split-flap headline

Use it when: a headline changes with the time or the season and the change is the news. Change: LINES (two rows of up to 12 characters from SET), the flap and letter colours.

mo-03 Letterpress proof

Use it when: a short statement should look pressed into paper: a manifesto, an invitation, a colophon. Change: the words, the two inks, the paper colour; raise scale in #mo03-ink for rougher type.

Set by hand, pulled one sheet at a time.

Sample copy · Two-colour proof, 1 of 40

mo-04 Gravity pile

Use it when: one sentence carries the page and should land with weight. Change: the words (5 to 10 read best), the block colours, G and C for heavier or bouncier blocks.

Plain words land hard and hold their place.

mo-05 Pen plotter

Use it when: the product makes, draws or prints, and the image should arrive the way it was made. Change: the paths (one stroke each, data-pen picks the ink), the ink colours, the pen speed in add().

mo-06 Line boil

Use it when: a menu, a kids' brand or a studio site wants hand-drawn warmth that never quite sits still. Change: the drawing and the line, scale on #mo06-boil, the three seeds.

Open late on Fridays

Sample hours · until 23:00

mo-07 Axis wave

Use it when: a headline should change character as the reader moves past it. Change: the line (keep MONO at 1 so nothing reflows), the axis values in @keyframes mo07-wave and in K.

Every letter takes its turn

Scroll: weight, slant and the casual axis travel through the line, then settle.

mo-08 Aperture iris

Use it when: a photo, a product shot or a portfolio piece should be revealed the way a camera takes it. Change: .art for the product's image, the blade count (step of --k is 360° / blades), the ring markings.

mo-09 Tri-vision billboard

mo-11 Flipbook

Use it when: a process has stages (growth, assembly, onboarding) and scrolling should step through them. Change: the drawing in the frame loop, N, the pencil colour.

mo-12 Word swap

Use it when: two plans, two readings or a before and after can be said with the same words. Change: SENTENCES (the same words in both; the self-check compares them), the colours.

Same nine words, two plans

Ship the small thing first, then make it good.

mo-13 Ink bloom

Use it when: a section should open slowly and quietly: an essay, a studio note, a chapter start. Change: DROPS, the ink and paper colours, the copy.

Field notes, issue four

Sample section. A drop of ink lands on wet paper and the block opens as it spreads. Swap in the product's own copy.

mo-14 Dither dissolve

Use it when: a product shot has two states (day and night, before and after) and the switch should feel printed. Change: the drawings in scene() or real images, the two palettes, the captions in TEXT.
