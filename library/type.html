type-01 Melting year (Climate Crisis)

Use it when: a year or a trend is the story and the headline should wear it. Change: the word, the year span, and what drives it (a date, a data series, scroll).

SEA ICE

The YEAR axis follows the Arctic sea-ice data its designers used, from 1979, with a projection to 2050. Climate Crisis · YEAR 1979–2050 · OFL · fallback system-ui with its weight falling 900→100 by year: offline the letters thin out instead of melting.

type-02 Chart in a sentence (Linefont)

Use it when: a figure deserves a chart but the page is prose, so the chart sits inside the sentence. Change: the sentence, the values (real, or marked sample), wdth to suit the series length.

Weekly sign-ups rose late in the week.

Sample values:

Each value becomes one character from U+0100 (0) to U+017F (127); the font joins them into a line. Linefont · wdth 25–200, wght 4–1000 · OFL · fallback ui-monospace: offline the line turns into accented letters, which is why the values also print in text.

type-03 Waveform from typing (Wavefont)

Use it when: voice, audio or messaging, where a waveform says "sound" before any word does. Change: the mapping (real audio peaks beat letters), the colours, the starting line.

Vowels draw tall bars, consonants middle ones, spaces near silence; a drawing of the text, not real audio. Wavefont · ROND 0–100, YELA −100–100 (held at 0, centred), wght 4–1000 · OFL · fallback ui-monospace: offline the bars become accented letters, so the typed line stays in the field.

type-04 Tilt with the pointer (Tilt Prism)

Use it when: a shopfront, venue or opening-hours page wants lettering that turns to face the visitor. Change: the words, the colour, the axis limits (±20 reads calmer).

Open Late

Move the pointer over the word, drag sideways on touch, or use the sliders. Tilt Prism · XROT −45–45, YROT −45–45 · OFL · fallback Rockwell, Georgia: offline the letters stay flat and lose the prism lines.

type-06 Scannable label (Libre Barcode 39 Text)

Use it when: tickets, labels, inventory, returns: anything that should actually scan. Change: where the code comes from (an order or SKU), the label fields; test with a scanner before shipping.

SHIP TO · BAY 3 · SAMPLE LABEL

Encoded:

Code 39 needs a start and stop asterisk, so the script uppercases, drops other characters and wraps the code. Libre Barcode 39 Text · no axes · OFL · fallback ui-monospace: offline the bars vanish and the code prints as plain text.

type-07 Pixel-snapped weave (Jacquard 24)

Use it when: textiles, crafts, heritage goods, where type should look made on a loom. Change: the words, the ground and thread colours, the range of multiples.

Hand Loomed

Sized only in whole multiples of the font's 24-pixel grid (CSS round()), so every woven pixel lands on whole screen pixels. Jacquard 24 · no axes · OFL · fallback Georgia: offline the pixels and the blackletter are lost.

type-09 Bus vinyl cut by scroll (Danfo)

Use it when: transport, street culture, long pages whose type should change as the reader travels down. Change: the words, the stripe colours, the scroll range.

type-10 Palette cycle (Honk)

Use it when: events, kids, festivals, where colour is the brand and can change per section. Change: the palettes kept, override-colors for the product's own colours, the MORF stop.

Street Fair

Palette 1 of 8
A COLRv1 colour font with eight built-in palettes, picked with @font-palette-values base-palette; the MORF stops are named in the font. Honk · MORF 0–45, SHLN 0–100 · OFL · fallback Cooper Black, Georgia: offline, or where COLRv1 is unsupported, the word draws flat white.

type-11 Keystroke burst (Kablammo)

Use it when: a playful input (a search, a name, a message) should answer every key. Change: the burst colour, the decay speed, the starting word.

WHAM

Each key adds 15 to MORF and it settles back to 0; under reduced motion each key steps the shape instead. Kablammo · MORF 0–60 · OFL · fallback Impact: offline the wobbling shapes are lost.

type-12 Copy of a copy (Workbench)

Use it when: archives, zines, music, any story about copies, leaks or reprints. Change: the words, the paper colour, how a generation maps to BLED and SCAN.

COPY OF A COPY

One slider drives two axes: BLED rises evenly with each copy, SCAN climbs faster near the end. Workbench · BLED 0–100, SCAN −53–100 · OFL · fallback Courier New: offline the bleed and the scan lines are lost.

type-13 Dot-matrix clock (Doto)

Use it when: transit, live status, anything where "now" is the content. Change: the data (a departure, a count), what drives ROND, the board colours.

LOCAL TIME · SAMPLE BOARD

00:00:00

Your device's clock; the dots go from square to round across each minute until you move the slider (fixed under reduced motion). Doto · ROND 0–100, wght 100–900 · OFL · fallback ui-monospace: offline the dots are lost, the digits stay tabular.

type-14 Copybook ruling (Playwrite DE Grund)

Use it when: education, kids, journaling, practice apps. Change: the line, the Playwrite country (each follows that country's school model), ink and rule colours.

type-15 Click to unredact (Flow Block)

Use it when: investigations, legal, privacy, launches with hidden details. Change: the memo and which words hide; never hide anything a sighted reader needs to act.

MEMO · SAMPLE COPY

The review moves to in . Bring and tell before noon.

Each block is a real button holding the real word, so screen readers read it; press one to reveal it. Flow Block · no axes · OFL · fallback: when the font does not load, a solid CSS bar keeps the words hidden.

type-16 Set in 1640 (IM Fell English)

Use it when: history, publishing, spirits, letterpress, anything older than the web. Change: the text and the rubric colour; keep the long-s rule rather than a blind find-and-replace.

The script sets the long s by the old rule: never at a word's end, not before f, b or k, not after f. CSS asks for common, discretionary and historical ligatures; the font draws the ones it has. IM Fell English · roman and italic, no axes · OFL · fallback Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Georgia: offline keeps the long s and drop cap, loses the worn 1600s print.

type-17 Layered sign (Bungee family)

Use it when: signage, food, nightlife: one word built like a painted sign. Change: the word, the four layer colours, which layers show.
