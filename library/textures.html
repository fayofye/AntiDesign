Textures

Specimens to re-cut, never to ship as they are: change the copy, colours and strength to the product's own. All of them are pure CSS, SVG filters and canvas, with no image files. The texture trait in scripts/roll.py names the specimen to open, or roll one with python3 scripts/roll.py --library textures.

tex-01 Halftone

Use it when: print dots should shade a shape or a photo. Change: the dot size, the ink, what is shaded.

Fizz & Pop

Grapefruit soda, poured at sundown.

Sample product: Fizz & Pop, a sample soda. Soft dots multiplied by a tone drawing, then contrast(24): where the tone is darker, more of each dot passes the threshold, so the dots grow. Draw the tone in greys (gradients, shapes) and the dots follow. Lilita One · OFL · fallback system-ui.

tex-03 Paper and torn edge

Use it when: the page should feel like paper: grain alone, or grain with a torn edge. Change: the grain strength, the paper colour, which edge tears.

Fieldnote Press · sample

No. 4 pocket notebook: 48 pages, sewn, not glued, with a ruled page for every day of a field trip.

Price on request. A sample product for a sample maker.

The grain is SVG feTurbulence in a data URI; the tear is a displacement filter on the paper layer only, so the text is never distorted. The paper runs 30px past the clip on the other three sides, so only the bottom edge tears. Keep the text at 4.5:1 against the paper you pick. Cutive Mono · OFL · fallback ui-monospace.

tex-04 RGB split

Use it when: a glitch should split one headline, not the whole page. Change: the word, the offset, the two channel colours.

SIGNAL LOSTSIGNAL LOSTSIGNAL LOST

Sample product: Static FM, a sample late-night radio station. The white word is the real text; two aria-hidden copies in red and cyan sit on it with mix-blend-mode:screen, so they overlap to white and fringe at the edges. The slice jitter runs only without reduced motion. Teko · OFL · fallback Arial Narrow.

tex-05 Hand-drawn border

Use it when: borders should look drawn by hand. Change: the wobble scale, the line colour and weight.

Little Sprout: Saturday cooking classes for kids aged 6 to 10. Aprons provided. A sample class from a sample school.

The border lives on its own aria-hidden layer with an feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap filter, so the lines wobble and the text stays crisp. Redraw picks a new seed. Patrick Hand · OFL · fallback Comic Sans MS, cursive.

tex-06 Dither

Use it when: a gradient or a picture should step in pixels, like an old screen or a riso print. Change: the two colours, the pixel size, what is drawn.

Lowres Weather

Sample product: Lowres Weather, a sample forecast app. The scene is drawn in greys on a small canvas, then each pixel goes light or dark against a 4×4 Bayer threshold and the canvas is scaled up with image-rendering:pixelated. Any canvas drawing works; a photo works only if it is same-origin, or its pixels can't be read. Pixelify Sans · OFL · fallback ui-monospace.

tex-07 Engraved hatching

Use it when: shading should look engraved, like a banknote portrait or an old catalogue plate. Change: the line angle and spacing, the shape it shades.

Ashgrove Mint

Sample product: Ashgrove Mint, a sample coin dealer. The same threshold trick as tex-01 with soft repeating lines over a shading gradient: darker tone, thicker line. Shade any shape by drawing its tone in greys under the lines. Rozha One · OFL · fallback Georgia.

tex-08 Crop marks

Use it when: the page should look like a print proof (publishing, printers, packaging). Change: the bleed, the colour bar, the slug line.

SAMPLE · halfmoon_tide-tables_cover.pdf · proof 2 · 27 Nov 2026

Halfmoon Books

The Tide Tables

A sample title from a sample small publisher.

The cover colour runs past the trim by the bleed; the crop marks stop short of it by a gap, at the trim lines. Registration targets, the CMYK and grey bar and the slug line are sample proof furniture: keep them decorative (aria-hidden) unless the slug says something true. Print bleed is usually 3 mm. Azeret Mono · OFL · fallback ui-monospace.

tex-09 Baseline grid

Use it when: a quiet page should show its rigour: every line on a shared baseline. Change: the line height and the copy.

A quieter month-end

Ledgerline reconciles every account against the bank feed before the books close, so the last week of the month is for review, not for searching. Sample copy for a sample accounting firm.

Every margin and every line height on this page is a whole multiple of one line, so the text on both sides of a spread, or in two columns, lines up across the gutter.

Body 16 on one line; the heading at 32 on two lines and this note at 13 on one are nudged onto the same baselines.

The script measures each block's first baseline with a zero-size inline-block and nudges it (position:relative; top) onto the grid set by the body text, then draws the grid from the same measurement. Text that wraps keeps the rhythm because every line height is a multiple. Source Serif 4 · OFL · fallback Georgia; the script re-measures once the font loads.

tex-10 Blind emboss

Use it when: a seal or monogram should be pressed into the paper with no ink (luxury, legal, stationery). Change: the mark, the paper colour, the light angle.

V&V

Vellum & Vane

Sample product: Vellum & Vane, a sample stationer. The seal is the paper's own colour, lit by one highlight and one shadow, so it has almost no contrast by design: it is decorative (aria-hidden) and the name is set again here in ink. Light from 225° is the top left. Marcellus SC · OFL · fallback Georgia.

tex-11 Duotone

Use it when: the product's own photos should share the page's two colours. Change: the two colours and the image.

Harbourside Rowing

Sample product: Harbourside Rowing, a sample rowing club. This drawing stands in for the product's own photo: put the photo in an <img> and give it the same filter:url(#…). The filter turns each pixel to its luminance, then maps black to the shadow colour and white to the highlight colour. Archivo Narrow · OFL · fallback Arial Narrow.
