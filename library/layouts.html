01

Centre

Week one: wedge the clay and centre it on the wheel until it stops fighting you.

02

Throw

Week two: open the lump, pull the walls up, and lose your first three cylinders on purpose.

03

Trim

Week three: turn the leather-hard pots over and carve each one a foot ring.

04

Glaze

Week four: dip, pour or brush. The colours lie until the kiln tells the truth.

05

Week five: the kiln opens on Saturday at 9 a.m. (a sample time) and you take home what survived.

DEEPLINE · a sample freediving school

  1. −40 m▲ Start here and read upward. The deepest point of the sample course: the line, the tag, the turn.
  2. −30 mKick again. Most divers feel the urge to breathe here; it passes.
  3. −20 mYou float on your own again. Long, slow fin strokes; save the effort.
  4. −10 mThe light turns from blue to green. Your buddy drops to meet you.
  5. −5 mLook up, keep the chin tucked, let a little air go.
  6. 0 mSurface: three recovery breaths, then the OK sign. Courses run at weekends (a sample schedule).

THE QUIET KITCHEN · a sample cookbook

The Quiet Kitchen

Recipes for one pot and a low flame.

A sample book; sample recipes.

Contents

  1. Broth, page 3
  2. Bread, page 4
  3. Pickles, page 5
  4. Index, page 6

2

Broth

Bones, an onion cut in half, cold water to cover. Bring it up slowly and never let it boil.

Skim once, then leave it alone for four hours.

3

Bread

500 g flour, 350 g water, 10 g salt, a pinch of yeast.

Mix at night, shape in the morning, bake at 240 °C for 40 minutes.

4

Pickles

One part vinegar, one part water, a spoon each of sugar and salt. Pour it hot over whatever the garden gave too much of.
