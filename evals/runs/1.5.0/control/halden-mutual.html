Good to know

What does AER mean?

AER stands for Annual Equivalent Rate. It shows what the interest rate would be if interest were paid and added to your balance once a year, so you can compare savings accounts on the same basis.

What does "variable" mean?

A variable rate can go up or down. If we change the rate on your account, we'll tell you.

How does a 95-day notice account work?

You tell us you want to withdraw money, and it's available 95 days later. In return for planning ahead, the rate is higher than our easy-access saver.

Are there any fees?

No. Neither saver charges a fee, and you can open either one with £1.
